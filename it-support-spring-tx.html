IT support for Spring businesses

Spring is an unincorporated community and Census-designated place in Harris County — it does not have a city government of its own. The Houston Northwest Chamber describes the Champions–1960–Spring–Northwest Harris County area as unincorporated, without a town hall, mayor, or main street, and says it sits about 20 miles from Houston's central business district. Postal Spring also reaches into Montgomery County, and the United States Postal Service lists Spring as the preferred city for every ZIP in this first set of local pages.

I-45 North is the commercial spine through Spring. The Spring Post Office is at 1411 Wunsche Loop, in ZIP code 77373. Old Town Spring is a historic shopping and dining district; it has a state-created Old Town Spring Improvement District under Texas Special District Local Laws Code Chapter 3813.

We also work with businesses in nearby Klein, The Woodlands, and Shenandoah. Oak Ridge North, a small incorporated city just north of Spring, is a USPS accepted city name on ZIP code 77386. Its City Hall is at 27424 Robinson Road, Oak Ridge North, TX 77385, which is outside this set of ZIP codes — 77386 is a large area and should not be treated as being entirely inside Oak Ridge North.
