FAQ

How long does this take to customize?

Most small teams can fill in the fields and make a first pass in a few hours. Getting an owner signature, an IT review, and counsel review takes as long as those people need. Do not skip the reviews.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The documents are written for owners and office managers. Where a step needs an administrator (for example, turning on MFA or disabling a departing user’s sign-in), the SOP says what to do and who typically does it.

Is this legal advice?

No. See the disclaimer below. These are practical templates, not a legal opinion, and not a security assessment of your business.

Will this get me cyber insurance or make me compliant?

No. A written policy can help you answer questions honestly and show your work. Your insurer, a regulator, or a customer decides what they accept. Using these templates does not guarantee coverage, a lower premium, or compliance with any law or contract.

What's the difference between Essentials and Complete?

Essentials covers the core policies and the procedures most shops need first: how people use systems, passwords and MFA, backups, incidents, and hiring and leaving. Complete adds remote work and personal devices, vendor access, and rules for AI tools.

What if I already have a policy or two?

Keep what is working. Use this pack to fill gaps, or replace a document that nobody can follow. You do not have to adopt every file.
