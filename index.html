Cybersecurity that small businesses can actually keep up with

Most trouble starts with a weak password, an unpatched computer, or a backup that wasn't really working. We help you cover the basics — and keep them covered.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

We help you turn on MFA for email, Microsoft 365, and other key logins, so a stolen password isn't enough to get in.

Endpoint protection

We put business-grade protection on your computers and keep it updated, so laptops and desktops aren't left on their own against ransomware and other malware.

Backups and disaster recovery

We set up backups you can actually restore, and a disaster recovery plan so a ransomware attack or a failed server doesn't take the business down with it.

Security awareness training

Your team is the first line of defense. We provide straightforward training so people can spot phishing and other scams before they click.

Compliance help

Need to work toward PCI or HIPAA requirements? We'll review where you stand and help you put the right controls in place. Book a free IT assessment to get started.
