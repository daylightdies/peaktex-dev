Why Peak Tech

Most small businesses don't need a full-time IT department. They need someone local who answers the phone, fixes problems quickly, and stays familiar with your setup. That's what we do.

We answer when you call

When something breaks, you shouldn't wait until the next business day. We offer 24/7 support, with 15-60 minute response times.

Straightforward monthly pricing

No surprise invoices for routine support. Our managed plans use flat monthly per-user pricing, starting at $70 per user per month, with a 12-month agreement and a $1,000 monthly minimum. We'll walk through what you actually need before you sign anything.

Example: 15 users × $70 = $1,050 per month. Teams of 14 or fewer pay the $1,000 minimum.

Local, and here around the clock

We're based in San Antonio, serve businesses across the San Antonio and Houston areas, and are a member of the Schertz Chamber of Commerce. You can call, text, or email us any time — 24/7, not just 9 to 5.

We catch problems before they stop you

We monitor your systems and send regular updates on the health of your technology. If something looks off, we'll tell you and fix it.
