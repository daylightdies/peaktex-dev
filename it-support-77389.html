About ZIP code 77389

77389 is a standard street-delivery ZIP code in Harris County. The USPS preferred city is Spring, TX; Klein and The Woodlands are accepted city names. This ZIP includes Springwoods Village. Exxon Mobil Corporation lists its corporate headquarters at 22777 Springwoods Village Parkway, Spring, TX 77389-1425. CommonSpirit lists St. Luke's Hospital – Springwoods Village at 2255 E Mossy Oaks Rd, Spring, TX 77389, open since 2016, with emergency, imaging, and outpatient surgery.

The Houston Northwest Chamber of Commerce contact block lists 1700 City Plaza Drive, Suite 385, Spring, TX 77389.

According to the U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 ZIP Code Business Patterns, 77389 had 830 employer establishments and 24,787 employees (employment figures include Census noise infusion; noise flag H, medium noise). That is the second-highest employment total in this 12-ZIP set, with fewer establishments than 77379, 77380, 77386, or 77388. By establishment count, the largest sectors were professional, scientific, and technical services (123), retail trade (91), and accommodation and food services (90).
